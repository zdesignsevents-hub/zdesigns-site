About Z Designs Events

Z Designs Events LLC is a luxury wedding and event design company based in Central Florida, specializing in floral artistry, tablescapes, and full venue transformations. We don't just decorate — we create immersive experiences that reflect each client's unique story.

Founded in 2019 by Haram Khan, Z Designs Events has become known for cultural fluency — designing across South Asian, Western, and fusion weddings with equal mastery. Featured in Maharani Weddings, we bring visions to life that others might call impossible.

When you book Z Designs, you get Haram. Not a subcontractor, not a junior associate. The person whose work you saw on Instagram is the person designing your event.

Frequently Asked Questions

The needs of every client vary and for that reason we focus more on the overall budget rather than the individual price of items and provide clients with a minimum spend.

At this time, our minimum is $4,000 per day or $2,000 per event after two events.

Because the needs of every client vary, the minimum is provided as a guideline on what to expect to spend at the least in order to produce an event based on our standards. So this minimum can include anything you need within the realm of our services.

We are a floral and production company that designs the visual experience for an event. Through our company we provide all backdrops, florals, centerpieces, furniture selections, custom builds to support decor and as needed will consult on various other services to ensure a cohesive visual aesthetic. Services outside of ours include but are not limited to: linen, napkin, charger plate, table top and chair rentals, dance floor wrap, room drape, lighting and stationary as well.

After submitting an inquiry through our website, we schedule a phone consultation with our creative director to build a proposal, with a swift turn around, we provide an estimate of what we suggest your spend should be based on your needs. If you are in alignment with the proposal you would submit a partial deposit to book the date and schedule your initial design meeting in which you design in person directly with our Creative Director.

We specialize in floral and event design and heavily rely on our creative partners in particular the wedding and event planners, we do not provide planning in house. The most successful events we see are the ones where the client has booked partial or full planning services.
